🧵 New field note: "Eventual consistency is fine — until you're moving money."

⚖️ Two payment service instances read the same $10,000 balance. Both approve a $7,000 withdrawal. Both commit. Nothing crashed, nothing threw an error — $14,000 walked out against $10,000 available.

💡 That's not a lagging replica catching up later. It's an invariant that should never have been eventual in the first place.
