The four digits
nobody sees

Two people order the same $42 dinner tonight. One earns 4x points, the other 1x — decided the moment their merchant signed up with an acquirer, not by anything that happens at the table.

One code, assigned to the merchant at signup, read three different ways off the same swipe — rewards, fraud and interchange each decide something different from field 18 of the same ISO 8583 message.
